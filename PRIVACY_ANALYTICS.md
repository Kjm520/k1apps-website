# Analytics & Data Collection

_Last updated: May 1, 2026_

Dynamic Mathematics ("the app") uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used so we can improve it. This section describes what is collected, what is not, and your choices.

## What We Collect

Through Firebase Analytics, we automatically collect:

- **Usage events** - which screens are viewed (e.g., Skills, Problem, Custom Select), when sessions start, when a problem is answered (correct or incorrect), the skill or topic involved, and the practice mode used.
- **Device & technical info** - device model, operating system version, app version, language, and approximate location derived from IP address (typically city-level).
- **Anonymous identifiers** - a Firebase-generated installation ID and an Apple-provided identifier (IDFV) used to distinguish one installation from another. These are not linked to your name, email, or Apple ID.
- **Engagement metrics** - session length, time spent in the app, and first-open events.

## What We Do **Not** Collect

- We do **not** collect your name, email address, phone number, or Apple ID.
- We do **not** require you to create an account.
- We do **not** collect contacts, photos, microphone, camera, health, or location-services data.
- We do **not** sell or share your data with advertisers or third-party marketers.
- We do **not** use the IDFA (advertising identifier) and do not show ads.

## Where Your Data Lives

- **On your device** - your problem history, progress, and statistics are stored locally on your device using SQLite. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to share it.
- **On Google's servers** - anonymous analytics events described above are processed by Google as part of the Firebase service. See Google's privacy practices at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

## Data Retention

Analytics events are retained by Firebase according to the project's configured retention period (currently the Firebase default). Local data on your device persists until you delete the app or use the in-app "Reset Progress" option.

## Your Choices

- **Reset progress** - clears your locally stored history and stats from within the app.
- **Limit ad tracking / analytics** - iOS settings under *Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking* and *Analytics & Improvements* affect data shared with developers.
- **Uninstall the app** - removes all locally stored data. Previously transmitted anonymous analytics events remain subject to Firebase retention policies.

## Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The analytics described above are anonymous and not tied to identifying information.
